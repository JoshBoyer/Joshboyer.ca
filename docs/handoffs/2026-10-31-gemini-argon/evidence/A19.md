### Defect D-01 (High — `RL-02` / `RL-03`): Desktop & Tablet `Trace` Tab Panel Is Blank Due to DOM Element Reparenting (`A16` + `A18`)
- **Observed Symptom in Chromium (`1440×900` and `1024×768`):**
  - After a conversation turn completes in `services/edge/chat-preview.html` (which loads both `/chat-request-details.js` and `/chat-assistant.js`), the active `#chat-trace-panel` inside `<aside class="chat-details-panel">` is **completely empty** (`visiblePanelTextLen: 0`, `innerHTML: ""`), even though `#chat-evidence-panel` (`888` chars), `#chat-tools-panel` (`62` chars), and `#chat-context-panel` (`337` chars) populate.
  - Visible in screenshots:
    - `C:\Users\boyer\.gemini\antigravity\brain\245bdc09-12ef-410f-b454-a388e311cca7\scratch\a19-screenshots\desktop-1440x900-trace-tab.png`
    - `C:\Users\boyer\.gemini\antigravity\brain\245bdc09-12ef-410f-b454-a388e311cca7\scratch\a19-screenshots\tablet-1024x768.png`
- **Root Cause (`dist/chat-assistant.js:1260-1297, 1306-1308` & `dist/chat-request-details.js:949-951, 1060-1061, 1277`):**
  1. `dist/chat-assistant.js:1260-1277` invokes `externalFactory.createDetailsPanel(bridgeConfig, bridgeConfig)` with `{ desktopRoot: desktop, mobileRoot, mobileDialog: dialog, ... }`.
  2. `dist/chat-request-details.js:1277` checks `isDomNode(options.container) ? options.container : createEl(doc, 'aside', 'chat-details-panel')` and **ignores `options.desktopRoot`**, creating a **detached, unmounted** `<aside class="chat-details-panel">` as its `primarySurface`.
  3. When `details.update(view)` runs in `dist/chat-assistant.js:1304-1338`:
     - Line `1307` mounts `view.timeline.element` into the visible `#chat-trace-panel` (`tracePanel.append(view.timeline.element)`).
     - Line `1332` calls `notifyExternal()`, which calls `externalController.update(view)` (`dist/chat-assistant.js:1294-1296`).
     - `externalController.update(view)` runs `renderIntoSurface(primarySurface, false)` -> `renderTraceTabContent` (`dist/chat-request-details.js:949-951`), which executes `if (isDomNode(timelineEl)) { wrapper.append(timelineEl); }` on the **detached** `primarySurface`.
     - Because a browser DOM `Element` can only have one parent, `wrapper.append(timelineEl)` **moves `view.timeline.element` out of the visible `#chat-trace-panel` into the unmounted `primarySurface`**, leaving `#chat-trace-panel` blank!
  4. The same DOM-stealing bug occurs in `renderEvidenceTabContent` (`dist/chat-request-details.js:1060-1061`: `wrapper.append(legacyEvidenceNode)`) whenever `citations.length === 0` (e.g., on general-conversation turns without portfolio citations), moving `view.evidence` out of the visible `#chat-evidence-panel` into the detached `primarySurface`.
- **Why Unit Tests Missed It:** `tests/chat-ui.test.cjs` runs without `dist/chat-request-details.js` loaded (`globalThis.PortfolioChatDetails` is unset except in test #41 where a tiny stub is injected), and `tests/chat-request-details.test.cjs` passes `{ container }` directly without `dist/chat-assistant.js`.
- **Required Fix (`A16` / `A18`):**
  - In `dist/chat-request-details.js:949-951` and `1060-1061`, do not reparent `timelineObj.element` or `legacyEvidenceNode` when `primarySurface.root` is not mounted in the document (or clone/render from stage data instead of stealing `timelineObj.element`).
  - Even better, align the bridge contract between `dist/chat-assistant.js:1260-1277` and `dist/chat-request-details.js:1277` so only one surface owner renders into `<aside class="chat-details-panel">`.

### Defect D-02 (High — `RL-04`): Mobile `<dialog data-chat-drawer>` Renders Two Duplicate Side-by-Side Details Panels, Causing Horizontal Dialog Overflow (`427px > 390px`) and `skipped` Stage Desync (`A16` + `A17` + `A18`)
- **Observed Symptom in Chromium (`390×844`):**
  - Clicking `.chat-view-details` (`View details ↗`) opens `<dialog class="chat-details-dialog" data-chat-drawer>`. Inside the open `<dialog>`, **two** complete details panels render side-by-side (`tabsCount: 8`, `panelsCount: 8`, `closeBtns: 2`):
    1. Left column (`x = 12..303.5px`): `<div class="chat-mobile-details-root" data-chat-mobile-details>` populated by `dist/chat-request-details.js` (`#chat-mobile-trace-tab`, `#chat-mobile-evidence-tab`, `#chat-mobile-tools-tab`, `#chat-mobile-context-tab`).
    2. Right column (`x = 303.5..491.5px`, clipped/overflowing): `<div class="chat-details-content">` appended into `dialog` by `dist/chat-assistant.js:1345` (`#chat-trace-tab`, `#chat-evidence-tab`, `#chat-tools-tab`, `#chat-context-tab`).
  - Consequently, `dialog.scrollWidth` is **`427px`** while `dialog.clientWidth` is **`390px`** (`37px` internal horizontal overflow inside the modal drawer).
  - Additionally, the left `mobileMirrorSurface` (`#chat-mobile-trace-panel`) shows all 8 canonical stages as `skipped / 0 ms — Stage was skipped for this request` even after all 8 stages completed!
  - Visible in screenshot:
    - `C:\Users\boyer\.gemini\antigravity\brain\245bdc09-12ef-410f-b454-a388e311cca7\scratch\a19-screenshots\mobile-390x844-drawer-open.png`
- **Root Cause (`dist/chat-assistant.js:1139, 1183-1185, 1345`, `dist/chat-request-details.js:813-815, 1293-1295, 1395-1400`, `dist/chat-assistant.css:1736-1738`):**
  1. `dist/chat-assistant.js:1183-1185` creates `mobileRoot` (`<div class="chat-mobile-details-root" data-chat-mobile-details>`) and appends it to `dialog`.
  2. `dist/chat-request-details.js:1293-1295` finds `[data-chat-mobile-details]` inside `dialog` and renders its 4-tab `mobileMirrorSurface` inside `mobileRoot`.
  3. Then, when `open(trigger)` runs in `dist/chat-assistant.js:1343-1356`, line `1345` executes `if (mobile()) { dialog.append(content); }`, appending `dist/chat-assistant.js`'s own `.chat-details-content` as a **second sibling child** inside `dialog` alongside `mobileRoot`.
  4. `dist/chat-assistant.css:1736-1738` defines `.chat-details-dialog[open] { display: flex; }` **without `flex-direction: column`**, laying out `mobileRoot` and `content` side-by-side in a row and overflowing `dialog.scrollWidth` to `427px`.
  5. Meanwhile, `toTurnRecord(view)` in `dist/chat-assistant.js:1139` sets `trace: Array.isArray(view.tracePoints) ? copy(view.tracePoints) : []` (an array of SSE trace events `{ stage, state, elapsedMs, detail }`), whereas `extractStageMap(turn)` in `dist/chat-request-details.js:813-815` checks `const rawStages = safeGet(traceObj, 'stages') ?? safeGet(turn, 'stages')` without checking `Array.isArray(traceObj)`. Because `Array.prototype.stages` is `undefined`, `extractStageMap` returns `{}` and marks all 8 stages `skipped`!
- **Required Fix (`A16` / `A17` / `A18`):**
  1. **Single Mobile Drawer Surface (`A16` / `A18`):** Either `dist/chat-assistant.js` must not append `mobileRoot` if it moves `content` into `dialog` on mobile open, OR `dist/chat-assistant.js:1345` must not move `content` into `dialog` when `externalController` owns `mobileRoot`.
  2. **CSS Flex Direction (`A17`):** In `dist/chat-assistant.css:1736-1738`, add `flex-direction: column;` to `.chat-details-dialog[open]`.
  3. **Trace Array Normalization (`A18` / `A16`):** In `dist/chat-request-details.js:814`, change `const rawStages = Array.isArray(traceObj) ? traceObj : (safeGet(traceObj, 'stages') ?? safeGet(turn, 'stages'));` and map `{ state: 'completed' }` to `'ok'` in `normalizeStageStatus` (`dist/chat-request-details.js:704-725`, which currently checks `raw === 'ok' || raw === 'pass' || raw === 'passed' || raw === 'complete' || raw === 'done' || raw === 'success'` but omits `'completed'`!).
